# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

A data analytics project evaluating retail sales performance, purchasing patterns, customer demographics, and revenue trends to drive business optimization.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This interactive dashboard aggregates transactional and customer data to deliver high-level executive insights and granular operational analytics. By examining customer retention rates, product demand, and demographic spending habits, the report empowers stakeholders to optimize marketing campaigns, target core age groups, and design retention strategies.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Product & Category Performance
* **Top Revenue Generator:** **Groceries** leads total sales, followed by **Clothing**, **Beauty**, **Home Decor**, and **Electronics**.
* **Consistent Volume:** Average basket size remains constant at **~2.5 items** per order across all product categories.

### 2. Customer Demographics
* **Gender Split:** Revenue is evenly balanced between genders, with **Female** customers driving **297K** in sales compared to **277K** from **Male** customers.
* **Age Distribution:** Middle-aged customers represent the core consumer base:
  * **31–40 years:** Highest overall spending group, particularly across Beauty (33,850), Clothing (28,650), and Electronics (25,550).
  * **41–50 years:** Second-largest spending group, showing strong engagement in Electronics (30,050).
  * **>20 years:** Lowest revenue contribution across all product lines.

### 3. Customer Retention & Behavior
* **One-Time Customers:** Account for **68.77%** (185) of the total customer base.
* **Repeat Customers:** Represent **31.23%** (84) of total customers.
* **Strategic Takeaway:** Converting high-value one-time buyers into repeat customers via personalized loyalty programs and email remarketing presents a major revenue growth opportunity.

### 4. Seasonality & Sales Trends
* Monthly sales peak in **May** (~50K+) before experiencing a steady decline over subsequent months, reaching the lowest point in **March**.

## 🛠️ Dashboard Components

* **KPI Header Cards:** High-level executive performance summary metrics.
* **Combo Chart:** Total Sales vs. Average Basket Size by Product Category.
* **Donut Charts:** Gender distribution & Customer Type breakdown (One-Time vs. Repeat).
* **Cross-Tabulation Matrix:** Sales matrix mapping Age Buckets against Product Categories.
* **Trend Line Chart:** Monthly total sales performance.
